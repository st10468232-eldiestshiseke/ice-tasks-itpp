Christoffel — Private Chef Menu App

A cross-platform mobile application built for a private chef who needs to update his menus for clients quickly. The chef can add and remove menu items (dish name, description, course and price), and the home screen automatically displays the full menu, the total number of items and the average price per course. A separate guest view lets diners filter the menu by course, such as starters, mains or desserts.

  • React Native
  • Expo Go
  • JavaScript
  • Cross-platform (Android & iOS)
