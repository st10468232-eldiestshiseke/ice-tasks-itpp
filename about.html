About Me

A short professional biography: who I am, why I chose IT, and where I am heading.

Why I Chose Information Technology

I chose Information Technology because it is one of the few fields where a single well-built solution can save people hours of work, connect them with others, or even start a business. What first drew me in was programming: the moment a program I wrote compiled and actually ran was the moment I knew this was the career for me. Since then, every module I have completed — from programming portfolios to UI and UX design — has confirmed that I enjoy the full journey of building software, from understanding what a user needs to delivering something that works.

My Career Goals

In the short term, my goal is to complete my IT studies with a strong portfolio of working projects and to gain practical experience through an internship in software or mobile app development. In the long term, I want to grow into a well-rounded software developer — someone who can design, build and ship quality applications, and eventually mentor others entering the industry.

My Interests Within the IT Industry

My main interests are mobile application development, web development and user-centred design. I am particularly interested in cross-platform development with React Native, because it allows one codebase to reach both Android and Apple users — which matters a great deal in a mobile-first country like South Africa.
